🎮 Game Mechanics

  • You have 3 Hearts (Lives). Wrong answers activate temple traps!
  • Answer 10 questions spread over 3 Tiers / Levels.
  • Correct answers award +100 Points plus consecutive combo bonuses!
  • Use Limited Hints if you get stuck in ancient chambers.

📚 Target English Grammar

Practice expressions for Asking and Giving Suggestions:

Asking Suggestions:

"What should I do?"

"What do you suggest?"

Giving Suggestions:

"You should..." / "Why don't you..."

"If I were you, I would..."
